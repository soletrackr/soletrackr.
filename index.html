A thin insole and a pod the size of a lighter.

The insole replaces your shoe's own liner. The pod clips into the arch, where your foot presses least.

Good questions.

Which shoes do they work in?

Nike, adidas, ASICS and Puma. Each pair is made to the size you choose and works in all four brands. Your size changes the price, not the compatibility.

Do I need my phone with me?

No. The pods keep up to a day of steps and sync the next time the app is open nearby.

Can I use it with my watch?

Yes. SoleTrackr tracks on its own and does not need a watch, but the two do not get in each other's way.

Is this a medical device?

No. SoleTrackr shows how you load your feet and does not diagnose anything. If you have pain, see a podiatrist. The Pro export is made to share with one.

Can I wear them in the rain?

Yes. The pods are rated IP67, so sweat, puddles and a quick splash are fine. Please do not swim in them.

What if they do not suit me?

Send them back within 30 days for a refund if they do not suit you.
